# SecureBank Funds Transfer Policy

This policy covers how money moves into and out of SecureBank personal
accounts: wire transfers, ACH transfers, internal transfers between a
customer's own accounts, and person-to-person payments. It states what each
method costs, how much can move in a day, and how long funds take to
arrive.

## Wire transfer fees

Wires are the fastest method and the most expensive. Fees depend on
direction and destination:

| Wire type                | Fee  |
| ------------------------ | ---- |
| Domestic outgoing        | $25  |
| Domestic incoming        | Free |
| International outgoing   | $45  |
| International incoming   | $15  |

A domestic outgoing wire costs $25, and incoming domestic wires are free.
An international outgoing wire costs $45, and an international incoming
wire costs $15. The fee is charged to the sending or receiving account on
the day the wire is processed, separately from the transfer amount.

International wires may also lose value to intermediary bank charges and
currency conversion, neither of which SecureBank controls or refunds. A
representative should tell the customer this before the wire is sent, since
the amount received abroad is often smaller than the amount sent.

A domestic wire requested before 4:00 p.m. Eastern time on a business day
goes out the same day. An international wire requested before 2:00 p.m.
Eastern time goes out the same day and typically settles in one to three
business days.

## ACH transfer limits

ACH is the standard method for routine external transfers and carries no
fee for either direction. The standard ACH limit is $10,000 per day. A
customer who needs to move more can request an increase to $25,000 per day,
which is reviewed against account history and granted for accounts in good
standing.

The daily limit counts the total of all ACH transfers initiated that
calendar day, not each transfer separately. A transfer that would exceed
the limit is rejected at submission rather than returned later, so the
customer can resubmit a smaller amount the same day.

Standard ACH transfers settle in one to three business days. Transfers
initiated on a weekend or bank holiday are processed on the next business
day and count against that day's limit.

## Internal and person-to-person transfers

Transfers between two accounts belonging to the same customer are immediate
and carry no fee or daily limit. Person-to-person payments to another
SecureBank customer are also immediate and carry no fee, with a limit of
$2,500 per day and $10,000 per calendar month.

## Holds and verification

A transfer from a newly opened account is held for the first thirty days,
during which the daily ACH limit is $2,500 regardless of any increase on
file. A first transfer to a new external recipient may be held for one
additional business day while the recipient details are verified.

A transfer out of an account with a frozen status is blocked, and the
customer is directed to account services rather than being given a
transfer-specific explanation. Outgoing wires above $25,000 require
callback verification with the account holder before release, and the
callback uses the phone number on file rather than any number supplied with
the request.
