# SecureBank Consumer Lending Policy

This policy covers the consumer loan products SecureBank offers to
individual borrowers: auto loans and unsecured personal loans. It states
the rate ranges a representative may quote, the underwriting floors an
application has to clear, and how payments and delinquency are handled. It
does not cover mortgages, home equity lines, or business lending, which are
written under separate programs.

## Rate ranges

Every rate quoted to a customer is an annual percentage rate (APR) and
depends on credit profile, loan amount, and term:

| Product                 | APR range       |
| ----------------------- | --------------- |
| New auto loan           | 4.49% – 12.99%  |
| Used auto loan          | 5.49% – 14.99%  |
| Unsecured personal loan | 6.99% – 24.99%  |

New auto loans are priced from 4.49% to 12.99%. Used auto loans are priced
from 5.49% to 14.99%, reflecting the faster depreciation of the collateral.
Unsecured personal loans are priced from 6.99% to 24.99%, since nothing
secures them.

A representative may quote the range for a product but must not promise a
specific rate before underwriting completes. The bottom of each range is
reserved for borrowers with the strongest credit, the shortest terms, and
the largest down payments; most approved applicants are priced somewhere in
the middle.

## Underwriting standards

An unsecured personal loan requires a minimum credit score of 620. An
application below that score is declined for the personal loan product,
though the applicant may still qualify for a secured loan against a
SecureBank savings balance.

Auto lending applies a maximum debt-to-income ratio of 45% including the
new payment. A used vehicle must be no more than ten model years old at
origination. Personal loan amounts run from $2,000 to $50,000, with terms
from 24 to 60 months. Auto terms run from 36 to 84 months, and the longest
terms are available only on new vehicles.

Applicants must show at least two years of continuous employment or an
equivalent documented income history. Self-employed applicants provide two
years of tax returns instead.

## Payments and late fees

Payments are due on the scheduled date each month. A payment received more
than fifteen calendar days after the due date is late, and a late fee is
assessed. The late fee is $39 or 5% of the payment, whichever is less, so
smaller payments carry proportionally smaller fees.

A borrower may change their due date once per twelve-month period at no
cost. Automatic payment from a SecureBank checking account reduces the
quoted APR by 0.25%, applied at origination and lost if automatic payment
is cancelled.

There is no prepayment penalty on any consumer loan. A borrower who pays
ahead of schedule owes only the interest accrued to the payoff date.

## Delinquency and hardship

An account thirty days past due is reported to the credit bureaus. At sixty
days the account moves to the collections queue, and an auto loan becomes
eligible for repossession at ninety days. A borrower facing documented
hardship may request a payment deferral of up to two months once per year;
the request is reviewed by the lending team, not by support staff, and
interest continues to accrue during any deferral.
