# SecureBank Account Fee Schedule

This schedule lists the fees that apply to SecureBank personal deposit
accounts. It is the authoritative reference for support staff answering
questions about what an account costs to hold and what individual services
cost to use. Fees are charged to the account that incurred them, and every
fee appears on the monthly statement with the date it was assessed.

## Monthly maintenance fees

Each account type carries its own monthly maintenance fee:

| Account type       | Monthly maintenance fee |
| ------------------ | ----------------------- |
| Premium Checking   | $12.99                  |
| Basic Checking     | $0.00                   |
| High-Yield Savings | $0.00                   |
| Student Checking   | $0.00                   |

The Premium Checking monthly fee of $12.99 is waived for any statement
cycle in which the account holder keeps an average daily balance above
$1,500, or receives a qualifying direct deposit of $500 or more. Only one
of the two conditions has to be met. The waiver is applied automatically at
the close of the cycle, so a holder who meets the condition never has to
request it. Basic Checking, High-Yield Savings, and Student Checking carry
no monthly maintenance fee at all, and so have no waiver terms.

## Overdraft fees

An overdraft occurs when a posted transaction exceeds the available
balance. SecureBank charges an overdraft fee of $35 per transaction, with a
maximum of 3 overdraft fees per day ($105). Transactions that post on a
weekend or bank holiday are grouped with the next business day for the
purpose of that daily count.

An account enrolled in overdraft protection has a linked savings account
drawn on instead. Each overdraft protection transfer carries a fee of $12,
which is lower than the overdraft fee it replaces. Transfers are made in
the amount needed to cover the shortfall and are reported separately on the
statement.

A transaction returned unpaid for insufficient funds carries the same $35
charge. Items presented a second time by the originator may be charged
again, because each presentment is treated as a separate transaction.

## Card services

Debit card services are billed as follows:

| Service                  | Fee        |
| ------------------------ | ---------- |
| Replacement debit card   | $5         |
| Expedited card delivery  | $25        |
| Card issued outside U.S. | $35        |

A replacement debit card costs $5. Expedited delivery is charged in
addition to the replacement fee when the holder asks for it.

## Other service fees

Stop payment on a single item costs $30. A paper statement mailed on
request costs $3 per cycle; electronic statements carry no fee. Research on
a closed account costs $25 per hour, billed in half-hour increments. An
outgoing cashier's check costs $10. Cash deposits above $10,000 in a cycle
are charged $0.20 per $100 on the excess.

## Fee reversals

A support representative may reverse one maintenance fee or one overdraft
fee per rolling twelve-month period at their own discretion, without
escalation. Any further reversal, and any reversal above $105 in total,
requires supervisor approval. Reversals post within two business days.
