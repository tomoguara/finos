# SecureBank Fraud and Unauthorized Transaction Policy

This policy covers transactions the account holder did not authorize:
card fraud, account takeover, and unauthorized electronic transfers. It
states what the customer owes, how a report is handled, and what support
staff may promise while an investigation is open.

## Zero liability

SecureBank applies zero liability for unauthorized transactions reported
within 60 days of the statement date on which the transaction appears. A
customer who reports in that window owes nothing for the disputed amount,
regardless of how the card or credentials were obtained, and no deductible
or share of the loss is applied.

The 60-day window runs from the statement date, not from the transaction
date, so a transaction near the start of a cycle effectively has longer.
Reports made after the window has closed are reviewed case by case and may
not be eligible for reimbursement, which is why staff should encourage
customers to review statements promptly.

Zero liability does not cover transactions the holder authorized and later
regretted, transactions made by someone the holder gave their credentials
to, or disputes about goods and services. Those are handled as merchant
disputes under a separate process.

## Reporting unauthorized activity

A customer may report fraud by phone, in the mobile app, or in a branch.
The representative taking the report blocks the affected card immediately,
before collecting any further detail, so no additional charges can post.
Blocking a card is not reversible; a blocked card is replaced rather than
reactivated.

The representative records the transaction dates and amounts in dispute,
whether the card was in the customer's possession, and whether the customer
recognizes the merchant. A written confirmation goes out the same day.

## Card replacement

Standard replacement cards are free. SecureBank does not charge for a
standard replacement card, whatever the reason for the request, and there
is no annual cap on the number of standard replacements an account may
receive. A standard replacement arrives in seven to ten business days.
Expedited delivery is a separate service and is billed under the fee
schedule.

## Provisional credit and investigation

Provisional credit for the disputed amount posts within ten business days
of the report, so the customer is not out of pocket while the case is open.
Provisional credit on a new account, open less than thirty days, may take
up to twenty business days.

The investigation completes within forty-five days for domestic
transactions and within ninety days for transactions made outside the
United States. If the claim is upheld, the provisional credit becomes
permanent. If the claim is denied, the credit is reversed with at least
five business days' written notice, and the customer may appeal once with
additional evidence.

## Prevention and monitoring

SecureBank monitors accounts continuously and may decline a transaction
that fits a known fraud pattern. A declined transaction generates an alert
to the phone number on file, and the customer can confirm or reject the
transaction from that alert. Staff must never ask a customer for a full
card number, a one-time passcode, or an online banking password, and must
never read an account's full Social Security number aloud.
